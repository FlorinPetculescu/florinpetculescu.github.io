I am Petculescu Florin, the individual developer of Seasons Unfold, published under the Bloomlight Games name. You can request deletion here even if you have uninstalled the game.

Request account deletion

Email me to request account deletion

Or send an email to florin.petculescu96@gmail.com with the subject Seasons Unfold account deletion.

How to request it

  1. Use the Google email address associated with your game account.
  2. Say that you want your Seasons Unfold account and associated data deleted.
  3. I may ask you to verify ownership. Never send passwords, verification codes or payment card details.

In the game, open Settings > Seasons Account > Request account deletion to reach these instructions. This is a manual request process: opening this page does not delete anything, and email drafts must be sent by you. I normally respond within one month; if a lawful extension is needed, I explain the reason and timing.

What is deleted

After ownership verification, I delete the game account and associated cloud progress, discoveries, villages, preferences, achievements, Coins, Hints and operational account records, and arrange deletion of the corresponding RevenueCat customer data. Associated purchase records are reviewed as part of the request. If a specific legal obligation, unresolved dispute or fraud investigation requires limited retention, I identify the retained categories, reason and period in my response; they are not kept for unrelated gameplay or marketing. Provider backups expire according to the provider's configured backup retention.

What does not happen automatically

This does not delete your Google account, erase Google's own payment or advertising records, or request a refund from Google Play. The request covers your game account; deletion does not cancel or refund a purchase automatically. A separate guest save may remain on your device: clear the app's local storage or uninstall it to remove that copy. Signing out or resetting gameplay is not account deletion.

Delete specific data without closing your account

You can also email florin.petculescu96@gmail.com with the subject Seasons Unfold data deletion, identify your account and specify which data you want removed. I explain any data needed to keep the account functional and any justified retention before processing the request. Request specific data deletion.
