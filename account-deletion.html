Delete directly in the game

  1. Open Settings > Seasons Account > Delete account.
  2. Read the consequences and type DELETE.
  3. If requested, verify your identity with Google, then confirm deletion again.
  4. Keep the game open until the deletion is confirmed and it restarts with fresh local progress. If a provider takes longer or cannot be reached, the game reports a pending or failed confirmation and lets you retry. Do not assume that closing the screen means deletion is complete.

No email to me is required for the in-game flow. It deletes the RevenueCat customer and then the Supabase account and associated game records. It clears the account's cached data and local gameplay save on the device where you completed deletion. Copies on another offline device may remain until you clear them.

Request deletion without installing the game

Email me to request account deletion

Alternatively, email florin.petculescu96@gmail.com with the subject Seasons Unfold account deletion, from the Google email used for the game, and state that you want the account and associated data deleted.

I verify ownership before processing web requests and normally respond within one month. If a lawful extension is necessary, I explain the reason and timing. Never send passwords, verification codes or payment card details.

Data removed and limited retention

Deletion removes your game account, cloud discoveries, village layouts, favorites, achievements, challenges, Coins, Hints, entitlements, gameplay operations and associated purchase records, and the corresponding RevenueCat customer. The only additional game-side retention is one-way hashes of fulfilled consumable transaction references for fraud prevention. These contain no account ID, name, email, progress or balance, are checked for 180 days and removed by daily cleanup within 181 days. Provider backup copies expire under the provider's configured retention. Removing active records does not promise immediate erasure of provider backups.

Local play and children

In Seasons Unfold Android build 9 (versionCode 9) and later, under-18 and undisclosed-age local play does not create an online account. To remove local progress and the privacy-mode setting, clear the game storage in Android settings. This does not erase an existing cloud account created previously. A parent can use the email request above for an existing child account without signing the child in again. The separate store-review demo does not import a personal guest save, and deleting that demo preserves the personal guest save.

Google account, store records and device copies

This deletes the Seasons Unfold account, not your Google account. It does not request refunds or erase Google's independent payment or advertising records. A remaining guest save on another device is removed by clearing that device's app storage. Signing out or resetting gameplay alone does not delete the cloud account. Purchases and their availability under Google Play's restoration rules are separate from account deletion.
