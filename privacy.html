Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07 · Android package: com.bloomlightgames.seasonsunfold

This policy explains how Petculescu Florin (an individual developer publishing under the Bloomlight Games name) handles personal information in Seasons Unfold, including guest play, Google sign-in, cloud progress, in-app purchases, and advertising when those features are available. Contact me at florin.petculescu96@gmail.com.

Children, families and age screening

In version 1.0.1 (Android versionCode 10) and later, the game requires a valid date of birth on a neutral screen before first access to gameplay. There is no skip option. The date is used on the device to select a privacy mode; it is not saved or sent to me or to service providers. Only a broad privacy treatment category and a screen-completion marker are stored on the device. Users who indicate they are under 18 use restricted local play. Missing or invalid classification returns to the age screen without deleting game progress. An already-screened player can choose stricter local privacy in Settings. Online account creation, Google sign-in, cloud synchronization, advertising, purchase SDK initialization and real-money purchases are disabled in that mode. Discoveries and village progress stay on the device. Browsing the local shop catalog and exchanging earned Coins for Hints do not require an online account or real-money payment. Android app backup is disabled. The game does not request the Android advertising ID or Android advertising-services permissions.

Online features are available only after the adult mode has been selected through the age screen. Advertising, purchase and account data described below relate to that online mode. There are no social chat, public user profiles or location-sharing features. A separate review code is available privately to store reviewers; it is not a parental-consent method and is not offered to children. No online child-account or parental-consent collection flow is provided. Parents can contact me to request deletion of information previously provided, including information from older app versions. If you contact support, do not include unnecessary information about a child.

Information processed

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google, I receive an account identifier and the profile information Google makes available with your permission, such as your email address, display name and profile image. Google handles your Google password; the game does not receive it.
  • Game progress: discoveries, village layouts, preferences, achievements, challenges, Coins, Hints and gameplay transactions needed to maintain your progress. Guest progress is stored locally. Signing in associates progress and eligible balances with your account.
  • Purchases: product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase status, entitlements, refunds, and account or app identifiers used to verify purchases. Google Play processes payment details. I do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Advertising and technical data: advertising providers may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device/app-instance identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic data (including SDK errors, loading time and performance). Providers may receive technical information when their SDKs communicate with their services, during eligible adult-mode use. The game does not require access to your contacts, microphone, photo library or precise GPS location. Ad requests are configured as non-personalized, with a maximum content rating of G; this does not mean that no technical data is processed. The game blocks Android advertising-ID permissions. A content rating alone does not establish child-ad eligibility. Advertising stays disabled for child, teen and unknown-age profiles while the current rewarded format and the necessary minor data-processing conditions remain unverified.
  • Support: information you choose to send me when requesting help or exercising privacy rights.

Why information is used

I use this information to provide accounts and gameplay, preserve progress, verify purchases and rewarded-ad completion, restore entitlements, prevent duplicate grants and fraud, troubleshoot failures, and answer support requests. When ads are available, Google AdMob processes information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention according to the choices and restrictions applicable to you.

Services that process information

The game uses Google Sign-In for authentication, Supabase for authentication and backend data, Google Play for Android payments, RevenueCat for purchase verification and entitlement management, and Google AdMob / User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent. These providers may act as processors or independent controllers for different activities. Information is disclosed only as needed to operate these features, comply with law, or protect the service.

Provider information: Google privacy policy, Google partner services, Supabase privacy policy, RevenueCat privacy policy.

Your choices and legal grounds

You can play as a guest without creating an account. Optional rewarded ads and purchases are not necessary for basic play. Where applicable, essential account and gameplay processing is necessary to provide the service you request; security and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interests; legal recordkeeping relies on legal obligations; and processing requiring consent uses your consent. Where required, advertising choices are collected before requesting ads. You can revisit available ad privacy choices through Settings and manage advertising identifiers in Android settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully performed.

Storage, security and retention

Local saves remain on your device unless cleared. Account and cloud data remain while the account is active. Account deletion removes the account, cloud progress, wallet, entitlements and associated operational records. To prevent previously fulfilled consumable purchases from being credited again, the backend retains only one-way hashes of those transaction references, without names, email addresses, account IDs, progress or balances. They are used for fraud prevention for 180 days and removed by daily cleanup, within 181 days. Provider backups follow their configured retention; independent Google payment and advertising records are governed by Google. Providers may process information outside your country, subject to applicable transfer safeguards. Connections to the game backend and purchase and advertising providers use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. Supabase states that hosted customer data is encrypted at rest. Native sign-in sessions use the operating system secure storage. Ordinary local gameplay saves are not separately encrypted by the game. This is not end-to-end encryption: authorized backend services process the data. Access controls restrict account data; no system can guarantee absolute security.

Access, correction and deletion

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to or receive a copy of your information, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact florin.petculescu96@gmail.com. I may need to verify account ownership before acting. You can delete your game account directly through Settings > Seasons Account > Delete account, with explicit confirmation and recent sign-in verification. The game waits for RevenueCat deletion confirmation before removing the Supabase account and associated game data. If a provider is temporarily unavailable, deletion remains pending and can be retried; it is not reported as complete prematurely. You can also use the account deletion page without installing the game. Web and specific-data requests are handled by me; I normally respond within one month and explain any permitted extension. Uninstalling the game clears neither an existing cloud account nor all provider records automatically.

Children and changes

The local-play protections above apply to children, teenagers and already-screened users who choose a stricter local profile. If you believe information about a child has been provided through an older version, support correspondence or another route, contact me so I can investigate and arrange appropriate deletion. I may update this policy as features or processing change; the date above identifies the latest version.
