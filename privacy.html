Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06 · Android package: com.bloomlightgames.seasonsunfold

This policy explains how Petculescu Florin (Bloomlight Games) ("we", "us") handles personal information in Seasons Unfold, including guest play, Google sign-in, cloud progress, in-app purchases, and advertising when those features are available. Contact us at florin.petculescu96@gmail.com.

Information processed

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google, we receive an account identifier and the profile information Google makes available with your permission, such as your email address, display name and profile image. Google handles your Google password; the game does not receive it.
  • Game progress: discoveries, village layouts, preferences, achievements, challenges, Coins, Hints and gameplay transactions needed to maintain your progress. Guest progress is stored locally. Signing in associates progress and eligible balances with your account.
  • Purchases: product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase status, entitlements, refunds, and account or app identifiers used to verify purchases. Google Play processes payment details. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Advertising and technical data: advertising providers may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. Providers may receive technical information when their SDKs communicate with their services, including during guest use. The game does not require access to your contacts or precise GPS location.
  • Support: information you choose to send us when requesting help or exercising privacy rights.

Why information is used

We use this information to provide accounts and gameplay, preserve progress, verify purchases and rewarded-ad completion, restore entitlements, prevent duplicate grants and fraud, troubleshoot failures, and answer support requests. When ads are available, Google AdMob processes information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention according to the choices and restrictions applicable to you.

Services that process information

The game uses Google Sign-In for authentication, Supabase for authentication and backend data, Google Play for Android payments, RevenueCat for purchase verification and entitlement management, and Google AdMob / User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent. These providers may act as processors or independent controllers for different activities. Information is disclosed only as needed to operate these features, comply with law, or protect the service.

Provider information: Google privacy policy, Google partner services, Supabase privacy policy, RevenueCat privacy policy.

Your choices and legal grounds

You can play as a guest without creating an account. Optional rewarded ads and purchases are not necessary for basic play. Where applicable, essential account and gameplay processing is necessary to provide the service you request; security and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interests; legal recordkeeping relies on legal obligations; and processing requiring consent uses your consent. Where required, advertising choices are collected before requesting ads. You can revisit available ad privacy choices through Settings and manage advertising identifiers in Android settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully performed.

Storage, security and retention

Local saves remain on your device unless cleared. Account and cloud data are retained while needed to provide your account and resolve operational issues. On a verified deletion request, we delete or anonymize associated account and gameplay data unless retention is needed for legal obligations, transaction records, fraud prevention or disputes. Any retained information is limited to those purposes; provider backups follow their own retention processes. Providers may process information outside your country, subject to applicable transfer safeguards. We use encrypted network connections and access controls; no system can guarantee absolute security.

Access, correction and deletion

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to or receive a copy of your information, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact florin.petculescu96@gmail.com. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. See account deletion instructions. Uninstalling the game clears neither an existing cloud account nor all provider records automatically.

Children and changes

Use of accounts, advertising and payments is subject to applicable age and parental-permission requirements. If you believe a child has provided information without the necessary permission, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. We may update this policy as features or processing change; the date above identifies the latest version.
